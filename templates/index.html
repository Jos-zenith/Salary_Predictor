How a prediction is made

01

Compute SaaS health

saas_metrics.py turns raw company numbers (MRR, churn, CAC, NDR) into ARR, LTV:CAC, Rule of 40, and a weighted 0-100 health score.

02

Predict the range

A quantile-loss HistGradientBoostingRegressor predicts P25/P50/P75 cash CTC from role, experience, city, and the health score.

03

Break it down

The median estimate is split into Basic/HRA/EPF/Gratuity per India's Code on Wages, with ESOP estimated and shown separately.

Where it's useful

Candidate evaluating a SaaS offer

An engineer gets an offer from a Series A cross-border SaaS startup with an ESOP grant. Before responding, they check the model's cash range for their profile, keeping the ESOP estimate separate rather than folding speculative equity into the number they compare against.

HR benchmarking a new role

Before opening a requisition, a People team runs their company's actual SaaS metrics through the calculator, gets a real health score instead of guessing off funding stage, and checks the resulting pay band against what they were planning to offer.

Limitations & ethics

Synthetic, benchmark-calibrated data — not live payroll

data/india_saas_ctc_dataset.csv is seeded and synthetic — real payroll data is confidential and not something this project has legal access to. What makes it calibrated rather than arbitrary: base pay bands, city premiums, and the cross-border gap are anchored to real 2026 India SaaS compensation ranges (cited on the Methodology page), and each synthetic company's health score is computed by the same saas_metrics.py functions the live calculator uses.

The live-signal feed is simulated

The terminal panel above and the ticker on the predictor are a labeled simulation (market_signals.py) — they demonstrate the event-driven architecture a real funding/hiring-signal integration would use, but are not connected to Crunchbase, Tracxn, or any live job-board feed.

No data retention
Inputs are used only to generate an instantaneous prediction. Nothing is stored or logged server-side.
SaaS-specific scope
Calibrated for Indian B2B SaaS roles specifically — not generic IT services, non-tech sectors, or executive compensation.
Reference point, not ground truth
Treat every estimate as one input to a negotiation or a budgeting decision, not a final answer.

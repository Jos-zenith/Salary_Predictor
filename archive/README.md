# Archive

Superseded by the India SaaS compensation engine (see the project root README).
Kept for provenance, not deleted:

- `EMPLOYEE_PREDICTION_ML_MODEL.ipynb`, `Salary Data.csv` — the original 6-week
  IBM SkillsBuild / Edunet Foundation internship project: a Random Forest regressor
  trained on a 373-row generic US compensation dataset, predicting a single salary
  figure in USD from age, gender, education, job title, and years of experience.
- `salary_model.pkl`, `scaler1.pkl`, `label_encoders.pkl` — that model's serialized
  artifacts. Not loaded by the current `main.py`.
